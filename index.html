TB3 Mapper

LiDAR robot maps for ROS 2 Nav2

TB3 Mapper turns a short walk with your iPhone or iPad's LiDAR Scanner into a map.pgm + map.yaml that ROS 2 Nav2 loads directly.

Getting started

  1. Mark the robot's start on the floor with tape. In the app, aim the + at it, point the phone the way the robot faces, and tap Set Start.
  2. Walk slowly, sweeping the floor and the bottom of the walls. Return to the start mark at the end.
  3. Tap Finish & Export, then AirDrop the files or upload them to your PC.
  4. Run ros2 launch turtlebot3_navigation2 navigation2.launch.py map:=$HOME/maps/home.yaml with the robot on the start mark.

Uploading to your PC

In the app, open Settings › Set up the PC receiver and send tb3_map_receiver.py to your computer. Run python3 tb3_map_receiver.py, then enter the address it prints in Settings › Upload to PC. Your phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi, and TCP port 8765 must be allowed through the firewall.

Troubleshooting

  • "LiDAR required": scanning needs an iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro model, or iPad Pro (2020 or later).
  • Walls look doubled: you moved too fast or tracking was lost. Scan again more slowly.
  • Tables block the map: lower the highest obstacle height in Settings.
  • Upload fails: check the Wi-Fi network, the PC firewall, and Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the iPhone.
  • Laser scan and map are rotated: set the initial yaw shown in the app (it is also written into the YAML).
